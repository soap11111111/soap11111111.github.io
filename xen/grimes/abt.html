claire allegedly began making music when she was assigned with learning the program logic for her electroacoustics class. she would begin posting music to her myspace in 2007, and proceed to become involved in her local indie music scene before getting expelled from college for skipping classes to perform. the year that claire dropped out would be the same year that she would release her first two albums: geidi primes and halfaxa. a year later, claire would release a split ep with fellow montréal producer d'eon, titled darkbloom.

after obtaining a dedicated cult following, claire signed to 4AD in 2012 - subsequently releasing her breakthrough album visions the same year. the album was produced using garageband in a mere 3 weeks, in which amphetamines were the only thing in claire's bloodstream as she didn't eat, sleep, or allow any light to enter her apartment. the album was an instant hit, with the songs "genesis" and "oblivion" gaining the most notoriety.

in 2015, claire would release a music video for the song realiti off a scrapped album, later refining the song for her 4th studio album - art angels. the same year would see claire's attempt to create a record label in hopes of promoting nicole dollenganger's music. a year later, she would collaborate with hana on a series of music videos called the ac!d reign chronicles, as well as producing a track for the suicide squad film (the emo one without the "the").
in 2018, claire sold out in the worst possible way with the announcement that she was in a relationship with notorious trillionare elon musk; becoming fiercely dedicated to whiteknighting him online, and using his dough to go through with numerous plastic surgeries. despite claire previously expressing her lack of a desire to reproduce, the equally deranged pair would go on to have three children - each given embarrassing, near-unpronounceable names - as well as spawning multiple fiascos; the most notable being the feud between claire and similarly controversial rapper azealia banks. despite having made herself into a laughingstock in the popular music world, claire was still able to release her 5th studio album - a "pro-climate change" album called miss anthropocene - to albeit lukewarm reviews, as well as landing a voice role in cyberpunk 2077. in 2022, boucher and musk would slowly begin to split apart, the former responding to this occurrence by staging a photoshoot featuring her reading the communist manifesto. claire would drop her feminist facade and continue to dive deeper and deeper into a sort of techno-accelerationist mindset, throwing her reputation in the garbage and all but entirely alienating her original fanbase in the process.

as of 2026, claire has announced the names of 3 supposed albums and one ep, all of which have yet to be released.
